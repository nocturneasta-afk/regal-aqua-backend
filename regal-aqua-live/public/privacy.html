Privacy Policy

Regal Aqua Premium Water Delivery Service

1. Introduction

Regal Aqua ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve our services. By using Regal Aqua, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and GDPR principles, we are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Definitions

Personal Data means data about a living individual who can be identified from those data, or from those and other information in the possession of the data controller, including any expression of opinion about the individual and any indication of the intentions of the data controller or any other person in respect of the individual.

Usage Data means information collected automatically when using Regal Aqua (for example, the duration of a page visit).

3. Types of Data Collected

A. Personal Data

While using Regal Aqua, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Email address, first name, last name, phone number
  • Payment Information: M-Pesa phone number and transaction IDs (payment details are processed securely)
  • Location Information: Delivery address, GPS coordinates for delivery tracking
  • Account Data: Login credentials, purchase history, preferences
  • Communication: Messages you send us, customer support inquiries

B. Usage Data

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Referral source
  • Interaction with website features

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors (Safaricom Daraja), email service providers, hosting providers
  • Delivery Partners: Courier services for delivery address and contact information only
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Successors: In case of merger or acquisition

6. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Our security measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing using bcryptjs
  • JWT token-based authentication
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • Firewall protection

7. Your Rights Under the Data Protection Act, 2019

Under Kenyan data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your data in a transferable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing for certain purposes
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: info@regalaqua.co.ke

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (For EU Residents)

If you are an EU resident, your rights under GDPR include all those listed above, plus:

  • Right to complaint to a supervisory authority
  • Right to object to automated decision-making and profiling
  • Enhanced data portability rights

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for the following periods:

  • Account Data: For the duration of your account + 5 years after deletion
  • Transaction Records: 7 years (required by Kenyan tax law)
  • Usage Data: 12 months (automatically deleted)
  • Communication Records: As long as necessary for support purposes

10. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional emails, SMS, or push notifications about new products, offers, and updates. You can opt-out by:

  • Clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in emails
  • Replying "STOP" to SMS messages
  • Updating your preferences in your account settings

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your login information
  • Track website analytics
  • Personalize your experience
  • Prevent fraud

You can control cookie settings in your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

12. Third-Party Links

Regal Aqua may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

14. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
